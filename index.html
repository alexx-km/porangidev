TLDL: Voice Message to Text

TLDL ("too long, didn't listen") turns voice messages into text on your phone. Share a voice message from your messenger with TLDL and the transcript shows up in a small floating window above the chat.

Transcription runs offline on the device. The audio is never uploaded. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. The app only goes online to download the speech model you pick.
